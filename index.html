Locations and products

List of Sydney suburbs

Sample list covering Penrith, the Blue Mountains and the Hawkesbury. The live version loads the full list from a maintained source and resolves each suburb to a Google Ads location ID. Targeting is set to people in or regularly in the selected locations.

List of all products

Each ticked product becomes one ad group, and its ads open that product’s own page. Deleting removes a product from this list only, not from your website.

3

Audience, budget and settings

Describe your customer and set the spend and schedule.

Customer profile

Google age bands: all ages.

Leave Male and Female both unticked to reach everyone. Google targets age in bands (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+). It cannot target a dollar income, so income is kept for planning and ad wording only.

Daily budget

Optional. Leave blank to run until you pause it.

Estimated monthly: enter a budget. Estimate only; actual Google Ads spend varies.

Campaign settings

Search network only: Search Partners and Display are off. The campaign and all ad groups are created paused. Set up conversion tracking (calls or form submits) in Google Ads to see which keywords bring enquiries.

4

Ads and ad groups

Write the ad template once. Preview how each product’s ad group will look.

Ad template

Use {Product} and {Area} as placeholders. Each ad group uses its own product page as the final URL. Lines too long for a product are skipped for that ad group. Only claim what is true, such as discounts or free quotes. Google reviews every ad.
