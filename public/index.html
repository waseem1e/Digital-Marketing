Audience, budget and settings

Describe your customer and set the spend and schedule.

Customer profile

Google age bands: all ages.

Leave Male and Female both unticked to reach everyone. Google targets age in bands (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+). It cannot target a dollar income, so income is kept for planning and ad wording only.

Daily budget

Optional. Leave blank to run until you pause it.

Estimated monthly: enter a budget. Estimate only; actual Google Ads spend varies.

Campaign settings

Search network only: Search Partners and Display are off. The campaign and all ad groups are created paused. Set up conversion tracking (calls or form submits) in Google Ads to see which keywords bring enquiries.

4

Ads and ad groups

Write the ad template once. Preview how each product’s ad group will look.

Ad template

Use {Product} and {Benefit} as placeholders — {Benefit} fills in automatically with each product’s key selling point (for example Duette shows as “Thermal Insulation & Energy Savings”, LumiShade and Silhouette as light-filtering and privacy). The ad itself always shows greaterwesternshades.com.au; each ad group also has its own product page as an additional link that it opens when clicked. Lines too long for a product are skipped for that ad group. Only claim what is true, such as discounts or free quotes. Google reviews every ad.
